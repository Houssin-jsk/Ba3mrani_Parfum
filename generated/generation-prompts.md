# Baamrani Parfum Image Prompt Manifest

All images used the local `Logo/logo.png` as the brand reference, the matching
`parfum_glas/*.png` as the exact bottle-family reference, and the matching
`parfum_refernce/*.png` as mood and color inspiration only.

## Shared Catalogue Constraints

One Baamrani rectangular clear-glass bottle with a glossy black cylindrical cap,
gold collar, and circular black-and-gold BAAMRANI PARFUM label. Centered,
full-bottle, seamless white studio background, subtle grounding shadow, sharp
realistic glass reflections, premium ecommerce photography, square format.
Original perfume bottle designs and branding must not be copied. No variant
name, volume, 20ML, 30ML, extra text, props, box, watermark, or checkerboard.

## Shared Hero Constraints

One matching Baamrani bottle as the dominant subject in a wide cinematic luxury
perfume campaign. Black and gold dominate; white supports; the perfume color is
only an accent. Realistic glass, refined shadows, premium reflections, clean
original composition. No overlay text, ad copy, variant name, volume, 20ML,
30ML, extra bottle, clutter, original perfume branding, or watermark.
